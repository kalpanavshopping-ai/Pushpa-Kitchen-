GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do I need to pre-book any items? +
Yes — specialized delicacies like liver, boti, paya, and prawns are prepared fresh upon order. Please pre-book them 1–2 hours in advance for small orders or 1–2 days for larger portions. Standard chicken and mutton dishes are available daily on Swiggy.
2. Can I order for a large group or event? +
Absolutely! We cater bulk family orders and small event gatherings (½ KG & 1 KG options available). Bulk orders require at least 48 hours advance notice so we can procure fresh meat and spices.
3. What are your delivery areas and timings? +
Our kitchen operates from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. We deliver locally across Asifnagar, Sitarambhag, and surrounding Hyderabad neighborhoods via Swiggy or direct home pickup.
